## Talk abstract

The talk presents the evolution of AI-supported software development, from traditional development assistants to Copilot and modern AI agents. Its focus is on how artificial intelligence participates throughout the development lifecycle: from eliciting and refining requirements, planning and programming to testing, documentation, deployment and maintenance. Particular attention is given to input quality, traceability, verification of results and the developer’s role in oversight and decision-making.
