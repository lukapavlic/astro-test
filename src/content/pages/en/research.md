## Agents are part of a larger engineering system

We do not study AI agents in isolation. We examine how agentic approaches interact with development processes, architecture, software quality, DevOps/DevSecOps, engineering governance, teams, users and organizations. Our focus is systematic, measurable and reusable engineering approaches.

## Research activities

### Development and evaluation of agentic development processes
We design and evaluate workflows involving coding agents and specialized AI agents, including responsibilities, context and verification.

### Tool, model and approach evaluation
We systematically evaluate coding agents, foundation models, agent frameworks, development environments, orchestration approaches and engineering workflows.

### Patterns and good practices
We identify, document and evaluate reusable interaction, orchestration and development patterns, engineering practices and verification approaches.

### Agent-ready repositories
We investigate AGENTS.md, project context, specifications, plans, architecture documentation, coding standards, skills, acceptance criteria and verification scripts.

### Experiments and empirical studies
Controlled and applied experiments examine correctness, quality, maintainability, effort, productivity, reliability, autonomy and context requirements.

### Prototypes and pilots
We develop prototypes and industry pilots to investigate engineering questions in realistic environments.

### Research transfer
We connect findings with practice, education, workshops, prototypes, pilots and good practices. Transfer also brings new research questions back to the lab.
