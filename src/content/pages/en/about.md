## Engineering, intelligence and people

The Information Systems Laboratory brings together engineering expertise, artificial intelligence and an understanding of people and organizations. It researches new approaches, methods, tools and technologies that improve the quality, development, deployment and management of information solutions and services.

Agentic Lab develops this work with a focused interest in agentic software engineering and AI4SE. Luka Pavlič is Head of Agentic Lab and Head of the Information Systems Laboratory.

## A broad foundation

**Quality and evidence.** Software and process quality, testing, metrics and technical debt provide the foundation for evaluating new development approaches.

**Architecture and delivery.** Software architectures, service-oriented and cloud-native systems, integration, agile and lean development, reference models, development frameworks, patterns and reuse connect design with implementation. DevOps, DevSecOps and engineering automation link this work to delivery and operation.

**People and organizations.** IT governance and auditing help place technical decisions in an organizational context. User experience, usability, technology acceptance, accessibility and digital inclusion keep attention on the people who use and build systems.

## From questions to useful practice

We aim to strengthen research in software quality, modern software engineering, AI4SE, agentic development and DevOps/DevSecOps. We develop prototypes and pilots, validate approaches in real environments and build R&D collaborations with companies and research organizations.

Scientific and professional events, education and reusable good practices help research travel beyond the lab. Our ambition is to become a recognized centre for modern software-engineering approaches, supported by careful evaluation and useful collaboration.
