## Start with an engineering question

A useful collaboration starts with your context: the system, the team and the problem you need to understand. Together we define a focused question, agree on evidence and explore a suitable form of collaboration.

We connect academic research with practical engineering challenges. The goal is a better understanding of what works, under which conditions, and how it can be reused responsibly.
