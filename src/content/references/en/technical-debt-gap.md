The study compares developer-reported technical debt with tool-based measurements in 14 production software products. It finds substantial differences between the two approaches. Automated measurements identify rule violations in code, while developer insight adds domain context and higher-level design considerations; the paper therefore advises against relying on measurement tools alone.
