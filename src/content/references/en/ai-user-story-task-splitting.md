A controlled experiment compares conventional task splitting with an approach assisted by GitLab Duo. The study reports that AI can help identify finer-grained tasks and reduce omissions, while human review remains necessary to remove irrelevant suggestions. Participants preferred combining AI assistance with conventional planning methods.
