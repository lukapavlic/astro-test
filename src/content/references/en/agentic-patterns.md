Candidate patterns include planner–executor, generator–reviewer, implementation–verification, specialist hand-offs, sequential pipelines, bounded autonomous execution, context hand-off and human approval gates. We investigate their conditions, trade-offs and failure modes. These are research topics, not universally established scientific results.
