## Engineering for people.

We examine how people experience, use and accept technology. Accessibility, inclusion and human–agent interaction are part of responsible software engineering.

Our research includes:

- User experience
- Usability
- Technology acceptance
- Digital accessibility
- Digital inclusion
- Human–agent interaction
