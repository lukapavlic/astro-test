## Engineering with agents. By design.

We investigate autonomous and semi-autonomous AI agents throughout the software lifecycle, with explicit responsibilities, useful context and verifiable outcomes.

Our research includes:

- Agent-ready processes
- Orchestration & specialization
- Sequential & multi-agent workflows
- Human–agent collaboration
- Context engineering
- Repository instructions
- Specifications & plans
- Agent skills
- Traceability & governance
- Verification
